**Recognisable real designs.** The Toyota Corolla, Hyundai Tucson, Audi Q7, Dacia Duster, Mercedes-Benz Sprinter and Fiat Ducato models are
real makers' designs. Badges and plates are removed, but the shapes are recognisable: they need a look by the lead / owner before any
public use. The three "Generic" models are fictional designs.

**`openx_next.glb` (Nov 10 2026).** A second, further modified conversion of the same nine works, plus `ox_cab`, which is the Toyota Corolla
model above with a cab's roof light and door lettering added. Modifications beyond those of `openx.glb`: lamp internals rebuilt (reflector bowls,
projector, bulbs, light strip), lamp covers and brake parts given their own materials, brake discs and calipers added where a wheel had none,
panel shut lines cut in, the places of the removed emblems closed, bars added in the Corolla's lower intake, surface normals repaired. The
credits, copyright lines and licences in the table above apply unchanged to every model in it, `ox_cab` included (same credit as `ox_corolla`).
Further modifications since the 14:02 build of Nov 10 2026: the outer surfaces subdivided and their normals recomputed, wheel-arch liners added, lamp bowls deepened and collared, daytime LED rows replaced by a light bar, grille sections drawn across the places of removed emblems, and a roof light, blank advert frame and chequer band added to `ox_cab`; credits and licences unchanged.
