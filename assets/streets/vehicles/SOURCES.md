## Our own
- `vehicles.glb`, `vehicles.json`: generated by this lane's scripts (`pipeline/models/`: `vehicles.py`, `cars.py`, `common.py`, `build_vehicles.py`, `run.sh`). Generic designs, no badges.
- Trade lettering: drawn at load on a canvas with system fonts; names and 555-01xx numbers are invented. Plates: plain, invented.

## Checked and not taken (Nov 9 2026)
- Khronos glTF sample assets: CarConcept (CC-BY, started from a Sketchfab model: ruled out), ToyCar (a toy).
- Gazebo Fuel OpenRobotics/SUV (CC0): a Hummer H2 with its badge in the texture: one recognisable trademarked design, left for the lead to judge. Bus, Ambulance (CC0): not converted yet.
- OpenGameArt, Kenney, Quaternius, itch.io CC0 packs: low-poly or PSX-style only.
- Poly Haven: one vehicle (taken). ambientCG: none.
- NHTSA / CCSA crash-simulation models: no licence stated on the pages; LS-DYNA meshes of real makes. Not taken.
- CARLA (CC-BY assets): Unreal .uasset only, needs Epic's engine to export. Not taken.
- Blend Swap, Smithsonian 3D, Wikimedia Commons, NASA: not searched in depth (Blend Swap needs a login to download).

## For the lead to decide
- `real_fprius` (Toyota Prius) and `real_fpickup` (Ford F-150 shape) carry no logo, but their shapes are recognisable real designs. Whether they may
  appear in the game at all is the lead's call (the owner's rule is no logos; the shapes are a judgement). They are OFF by default
  (`?vreal=1` turns the real kinds on); with the switch off nothing from `real.glb` is loaded or drawn.

## openx-assets (allowed by CHIEF, Nov 9 22:5x; `?vopenx=1`, off by default)
`openx.glb` / `openx.json`: nine models from OpenX Assets (https://github.com/vevalabs/openx-assets, release 20261015, GitHub download only),
MPL-2.0 AND CC-BY-4.0; authors and copyright lines per model in ATTRIBUTION.md. Converted by `pipeline/models/convert_openx.py`
(Blender 4.5, laptop 3) from `pipeline/models/openx_src/`. Changes: materials renamed to the kit's; the models' plates replaced by a plain
invented one; small bright parts at the middle of nose and tail (badges, maker script) deleted; a cab roof light and a lettering panel
added where the kind needs one; two lighter copies made by decimation (about 6,400 and 1,500 triangles); one wheel kept as its own part.
**Recognisable real designs** (Corolla, Tucson, Q7, Duster, Sprinter, Ducato): need a look before any public use. Blind judges decide
whether they replace the hand-built cars; until then the switch stays off.

`openx_next.glb` / `openx_next.json` (Nov 10; not loaded by the live game until the cars loop wires it in): the same nine OpenX Assets models, further
modified, plus `ox_cab` (the Corolla model dressed as a New York cab). Built by `pipeline/models/convert_openx_next.py` (with `next_parts.py`,
`next_views.py`; run by `badges_next.sh`). Changes on top of the ones above: lamp covers the models made of window glass are `lens`; the reflector
surfaces are a dark `lamp_bezel` and each round beam reflector is rebuilt as a smooth chrome bowl with a projector or a bulb in it; daytime LEDs are
`drl`, with a strip added under a row of them; brake discs and calipers get their own materials and are added to the wheels that had none; the
outlines of doors, bonnet and boot are stepped in with a near-black wall and floor (`gap`), and a filler-flap outline is drawn on the left rear wing;
the place of a removed emblem is closed (chrome bars run across on the Corolla, a plain plate on Duster and Sprinter; the chrome lozenge is gone);
the Corolla's lower intake gets bars with relief; bad stored normals on the paint are recomputed. `ox_cab` adds a roof light, the words
"NYC TAXI" and an invented number "7K42" on the front doors and a rate-card outline on the rear ones (Blender's built-in font; no operator,
agency or maker name or logo). No new third-party material. Credits and licences are unchanged (ATTRIBUTION.md).
Since the 14:02 build of Nov 10 the near model of every kind is also subdivided once (open panel borders and edges sharper than 35 degrees creased, normals recomputed), with dark wheel-arch liners, deeper lamp bowls behind the cover with a chrome collar, one light bar for a row of daytime LEDs, the grille drawn across where an emblem sat (`ox_sprinter`, `ox_q7`, `ox_ducato`), and on `ox_cab` a rounded roof light, a blank roof-advert frame (`rack_ad` / `rack_bar`) and a chequer band on the front doors; all of it made in the converter, no new third-party material.
